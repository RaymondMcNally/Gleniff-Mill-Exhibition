ARCT06004 Architecture Design Studio 1 · Project 1 · Stage 1

The Waters and the Wild.

You'll design a temporary, sustainable pavilion for shelter, play and education for children at the old Barytes Mill in Gleniff. Stage 1 is about getting to know the place. This site turns the brief into a working checklist: what to produce, what to look for, and where to find help.

03 · Base drawings

Underlays for Task C

Print these at 100% and trace over them. They're underlays, not finished drawings: your base drawings are drawn by you, in freehand black ink. Contours are at 1 m from the drone lidar survey.

PDF · A21:500 · sheet 1 of 2 · westMill, river and the west half of the survey.
PDF · A21:500 · sheet 2 of 2 · eastThe gorge and the slopes above it.
PDF · A11:500 · whole surveyThe full survey on one sheet, for reference and group work.
PDF · A31:20,000 · location plan templateFrame, scale bar, north point and the site to scale. You add the landscape.
The whole survey is about 394 × 184 m, which at 1:500 is 788 × 368 mm. That's wider than an A2 sheet, so the 1:500 plan is rotated to fit the site's length and split over two sheets. The north point shows the rotation. Check the brief's site selection boundary (p.2) against the sheets and work on the part that holds your site.
For the 1:20,000 plan, use GeoHive or the Tailte Éireann map viewers to trace the landscape around Gleniff. Check your scale: a 1 km grid square must measure 50 mm on the sheet.
04 · Sustainable Development Goals

Five goals, read through this site

The brief asks you to consider the site in light of these UN Sustainable Development Goals. Use the questions to start your notes.

3Good health and well-beingWhat does this place do for a child's body and mind? Where would they run, climb, rest?
12Responsible consumption and productionWhat materials are already here? What could a temporary pavilion borrow and give back?
13Climate actionHow do sun, wind and rain shape where and how you'd build?
14Life below waterWhat lives in the river? How would building near it affect the water?
15Life on landWhich habitats are on site, and what would your pavilion disturb or protect?
