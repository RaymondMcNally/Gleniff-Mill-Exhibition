What students can see

Switch parts of the site on as the project moves on. A switch that's off hides that page from the menu and shows a "not open yet" notice instead; a format that's off disappears from Your model. Give an item an opening date and it switches itself on that morning. Changes reach students when you publish.

This paces what students see; it isn't a password. Anyone who knows a page's address, or looks in the GitHub repository, can still open the files. The exhibition kiosk and the windows it drives always stay open.
